Working at The Merc and Sangre de Cristo Community Enterprises

The Merc is a for-profit cooperative in support of community, innovation, incubation and enterprise in support of its interns and members to provide for basic needs while creating a foundation for sustainable, resilient lifestyles in community and peripheral to community.

“Interns” are required to work 4 hours per day 6 days per week on a variety of community projects in exchange for room, board, educational experience and the dynamic, co-creative, out of the box thinking that is The Merc and Sangre de Cristo Communities approach to living on the edge of the 21st century. Interns may work within The Merc/Sangre de Cristo Enterprises after their 24 hours per week is satisfied. They may also work outside of The Merc/Sangre de Cristo Enterprises provided that they complete their 24 hours per week each week. Exceptionally and intern may take on an outside job/project that requires an entire week of focus outside of Merc/SDCC provided that the 24 hours is made up the following week. Two weeks back to back like this will not be acceptable.

Members are individuals or organizations that have invested the initial capital in The Merc, and/or have made an “in-kind” contribution of labor, equipment, supplies, etc… Members must be approved by a 2/3 majority.

Members may increase their capital accounts through contribution of money or through labor at market rates for their skill level, or be compensated with cash payments weekly on Friday (or when possible when cash is thin) for work performed that week.

Assignees/ Invited Contributors

“Assignees” are individuals or organizations that have contributed money of “in kind” labor, equipment, supplies, etc… Assignees can, if they desire be considered for membership in The Merc if elected by 2/3 majority of the Members.

“Invited contributors” (IC) are individuals or organizations that may contribute labor, materials, expertise, etc…. as investment to The Merc.

IC’s may contribute all or part of their labor on a weekly basis.

Capital accounts will be maintained for all members Assignees and Invited Contributors.

If the various communities do not have the $ to pay for labor, labor can be invested, if person/entity is an Invited Contributor

No debt will ever be accepted unless approved by managing member’s unanimous consent and no other options are available. As communities, we are allergic to debt.

To every extent possible, we will structure our transactions to be outside of government imposition of taxes, fees, fines, surcharges, penalties, etc….

We will pay all lawful taxes.

We will assiduously avoid al “legal” but not “lawful taxes”, fees, etc…..

We will use all legal structural mechanisms such as cooperatives, non-profits, bartering, cashless transactions, etc…..outside of regular taxable cash transactions requiring reporting and filing of “returns” to every extent possible.
