about bodywork

benefits of bodywork

Bodywork is a form of healing that works with the subtle energies of the body as well as the physical muscles, fascia, and connective tissue. The physical body reflects perfectly the state of our emotions and spirit. Bodywork can support the release of stored tension, old emotions, and limiting belief structures. It is a powerful way to balance the body’s energy systems and bring about peace and wholeness.

  • Eases mental and physical stress by releasing painful areas of tension and soothing the spirit.
  • Promotes wellness in the body by re-training the muscles to remain relaxed and fluid.
  • Increases circulation of blood and lymph throughout the body.
  • Increases range of motion and flexibility.
  • Promotes deeper and easier breathing.
  • Relieves tension and supports overall vitality.

Authentic Presence — Finn’s deep therapeutic touch is the result of his practice of being, rather than doing. By being fully present in mind, body and spirit, he is able to bring deep communication and intuitive understanding of his client’s needs and desires. You too will experience ease, fluidity, openness, lightness, and the deep relaxation of simply being.
