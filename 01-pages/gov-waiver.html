Waiver and Release of Liability

We hope you enjoy your adventure at Streaming Light, Silent Fire Ranch, St. Mary’s Field, and The Merc!!

Whether you have come to the Streaming Light, Silent Fire Ranch, St. Mary’s Field, and The Merc to participate in specific activities or just to unwind and learn more about yourself, we want your stay here to be relaxing, pleasant and above all SAFE.

Streaming Light and Silent Fire Ranch are rustic, wild and natural sites. The Merc is an industrial building, offices, and school and artist studios. St. Mary’s Field is an urban farm in Walsenburg. We have taken all reasonable precautions to make activities safe for participants. However, participation in any outdoor activity and creative work at The Merc is potentially dangerous. You may be exposed to risks of property loss or damage, personal injury and possibly even death while at Streaming Light, Silent Fire Ranch, St. Mary’s Field and The Merc.

Following these simple rules will greatly reduce the risk of accidents and injuries during your stay at Streaming Light, Silent Fire Ranch, St. Mary’s Field, and The Merc.
