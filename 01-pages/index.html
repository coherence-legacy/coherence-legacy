In architecture and design, coherence is the degree to which individual elements, spaces, or components fit together into a clear, logical, and unified whole. It reflects how well parts align around shared ideas, patterns, and relationships rather than feeling disconnected or contradictory.

In metaphysical thought, coherence refers to a state of harmony, alignment, and unity across mind, body, spirit, and reality itself. Rather than treating existence or the self as fragmented parts, metaphysical frameworks view coherence as wholeness, resonance, and connectedness.

  • A state where thoughts, emotions, intentions, and awareness operate in sync rather than inner conflict
  • A felt sense of unity with a larger field of consciousness, existence, or "Source," often described as non-duality or oneness
  • In speculative metaphysical ontologies, coherence is proposed as an underlying ordering principle or "fabric" that holds forms, perception, or reality together.
