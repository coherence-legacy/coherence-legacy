tantric massage

Tantric massage works with the subtle energy of the body, supporting the release of stored tension and the movement of life force. It is characterized by presence, breath, and a quality of touch that invites the client into a deeper state of relaxation and awareness.

finn’s way

In Tantric work I move slowly, staying fully present to the client’s breath and energy. The intention is not stimulation but the cultivation of ease, openness, and the experience of being fully met. Sessions are long enough for the body to soften and for the client to drop beneath the thinking mind into a more direct experience of sensation and presence. All processes flow with the client’s breath. The work may include elements of Esalen, deep tissue, and continuous energy scanning while remaining in a state of absolute presence and grace.
