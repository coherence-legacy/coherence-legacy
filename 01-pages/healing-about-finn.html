Finn Jorgensen, CMT

my path to the healing arts

I began my massage practice 25 years ago as a triathlete training with two top triathletes, both massage therapists. Over the five years we trained together, they taught me to do Deep Tissue and Sports Massage. In order to keep our bodies functioning at optimal levels we all exchanged massage regularly. I went on to a career in architecture and interior design but never stopped doing massage on athletes, friends and family.

Over the past five years my path towards freedom and “right livelihood” has led me to an increasing integration of design and the healing arts. I have studied at the world-renowned Watsu Center in northern California, from which I have received Advanced Bodywork Practitioner Certification. I am certified in: Swedish/Esalen, Advanced Therapeutic Massage (including Myofascial Release, Neuromuscular Therapy, Assisted Stretching and Joint Mobilization), Deep Tissue, and Reflexology. My energy work integrates Shiatsu, Quantum Healing and Tonglen.

Over the years I have regularly received a variety of massage and bodywork modalities in order to help my posture from leaning over a drafting board or sitting in front of a computer all day. Massage helped reduce stress, keep my body as flexible and fluid as possible, and most important it helped me to stay in touch with a body that has served me well, but is often neglected and under rewarded for its excellent performance. I received Swedish, Deep Tissue, Sports, Reflexology, Myofascial Release, Neuromuscular Therapy, Shiatsu, Rolfing, Trager, Tantric, Thai, Trigger Point, Hot Stone Massage, Orthobionomy, Reiki, Watsu, Water Dance, Acupressure, Acupuncture, Craniosacral, and several other more esoteric modalities.

I have become something of a connoisseur of massage, and various bodywork modalities, always seeking out the most skilled practitioners known for their intuitive and technical abilities. For me bodywork has been a major part of my path towards peace, joy and well-being. I am sure once you have experienced my work that you will agree that my years of experience will help you too towards optimal health and well-being.
