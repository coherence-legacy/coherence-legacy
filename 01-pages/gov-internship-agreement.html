Mercologist Agreement

As a Mercologist:

I agree to The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch and at achieve its stated goals and mission.

Daily I agree to bring the values outlined in the SDCC Field of Alliance Agreements to my experience at The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch.

I will participate in an inclusive democratic decision making structure of The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch, and at The Merc government (or consciously waive my right to express my opinions) and abide by the decisions made, including those made before my membership in The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch. I understand that a democratic government depends on the integrity of its participants and their commitment to the ideals of trust, respect, self-empowerment, cooperation, equal access to power, and non-violence.

I agree to give up my membership upon request of the members, at their will and for any reason(s), according to the process outlined in Silent Fire Ranch and The Merc Bylaws section 9b.

I agree to resolve conflict peacefully and be open to the use of mediation.

I am committed to pioneering a lifestyle that will serve as an example of ecological sustainability, as explained in the Sustainability Guidelines. I will weigh the implications of my actions, continually striving to minimize my negative impact and increase my positive impact on the Earth. I will participate in the creation of a physical and social structure and culture that will move us towards long term sustainability.

I am committed to the vision of diverse membership of The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch and will not discriminate against any person on the basis of race, ethnicity, religion, sex, sexual orientation, creed, age, ability, educational level, or economic background. I will attempt to minimize barriers to members of minority groups wishing to participate.

I understand that part of the The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch mission is to serve as an example of cooperation, sustainable living, local focus, emotional and spiritual evolution through outreach and education. I understand that The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch will continually pursue this goal by being open to new interns and members (as resources allow), guests, and visitors and by publicizing the actions and activities of our community.

This agreement does not bestow any form of ownership or residential membership in The Merc (SGMB LLC) or Silent Fire Ranch (SDCC LLC). Sangre de Cristo Community is renting space from The Merc. At this time, it is unclear how, and if, SFR will be allowed any additional residential capacity of two residences maximum per the Majors Ranch CC&R’s and Huerfano County Zoning. Like The Merc, Silent Fire Ranch (SDCC LLC) is owned by its investors and governed by separate operating agreements. Any Residential situations at The Merc and SFR are strictly month to month. At this time, SFR is to be resource for the Sangre de Cristo Community to establish resilience and autonomy from Walsenburg if and when desired.

Intern Volunteering and Income Opportunities

Participation in income opportunities at The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch are limited at this early time in our evolution. In general members residing for a limited time (not permanently), it is expected that you will work at the various projects underway. For residents at The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch, your cost of room and board can be satisfied by working for a minimum of 4 hours per day six days per week. If additional paid work is available at The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch Members may earn $10 per hour. Work outside of The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch is also possible provided that Interns provide 4 hours per day 6 day per week for room and board.

For clarity, The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch vision/mission is included here:

SDCC LLC (The Merc, St Mary’s Field & Silent Fire Ranch) Mission Statement

Through education and a cooperative support structure, we seek to create a society, made up of individuals and communities of various sizes and social structures, which allows and encourages its members to provide for their basic needs of shelter, food and staying safe. Then to encourage this society to grow, prosper and to have the size and recognition necessary to have an influence on the global community by example, education, and research.

Create a working model of a sustainable*, conscious, cooperative, resilient, low cost, debt free community. A community that is dedicated to attracting young and young-at-heart people back to America’s heartland. A community that supports a kinder, gentler, more meaningful, small town, locally-oriented agrarian, artistic and mercantile life.

*Sustainably: In such a manner that, within the defined area, no resources are consumed faster than their natural replenishment, and the enclosed system can continue indefinitely without degradation of its internal resource base or the standard of living of the people and the rest of the ecosystem within it, and without contributing to the non-sustainability of ecosystems outside.
