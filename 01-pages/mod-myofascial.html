myofascial release

Myofascial release addresses the connective tissue (fascia) that surrounds and interconnects muscles, organs, and structures throughout the body. Restrictions in the fascia can create patterns of pain and limited movement that are not resolved by muscle work alone. The technique uses sustained pressure and gentle stretching to encourage the fascia to lengthen and release.

finn’s way

I work with the fascia in long, slow holds, waiting for the tissue to respond rather than forcing a change. Areas of particular attention include:

  • Hamstrings to pelvic floor
  • Biceps and triceps to elbow and shoulder
  • Forearm to elbow
  • And other regions as the tissue indicates

The work is patient and responsive to what the tissue is willing to do in the moment. Myofascial release is often combined with deep tissue and neuromuscular work for profound therapeutic results focused area by area.
