healing touch

Healing Touch is an energy-based approach that supports the body’s natural healing process. Practitioners use the hands, on or near the body, to clear, balance, and strengthen the energy field. Related modalities such as Reiki work with similar principles of intentional, non-invasive energy support.

finn’s way

Energy work is integral to everything I do. Whether I am working with my hands on the body or holding space near it, the intention is the same: to support the free flow of energy and the client’s own capacity to heal.

Energy work skills that continue to be important include:

  • Reconnecting with energy healing
  • Learning to truly ground
  • Extracting without getting caught
  • Digging deep into teachings such as those of Jeffrey Allen

The work may involve continuous energy scanning and flow throughout a session. In some cases extraction is appropriate if it can be immediately grounded. The guiding principle is always presence and the client’s well-being.
