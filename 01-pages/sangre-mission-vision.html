sdcc llc mission statement

Create a working model of a cooperative, low cost, sustainable, resilient, debt free community dedicated to attracting young and young-at-heart people back to America’s heartland and a kinder, gentler, more meaningful small town, locally-oriented agrarian life.

We seek to create a society, made up of individuals and communities of various sizes and social structures, which allows and encourages its members to live in cooperation, sustainably, with local resilience, with ongoing emotional and spiritual evolution, focused on local solutions to the evolution of an enlightened social, economic and ecologically sustainability society.

Encourage this society to grow and to have the size and recognition necessary to have an influence on the global community by example, education, and research.

Sustainably: In such a manner that, within the defined area, no resources are consumed faster than their natural replenishment, and the enclosed system can continue indefinitely without degradation of its internal resource base or the standard of living of the people and the rest of the ecosystem within it, and without contributing to the non-sustainability of ecosystems outside.

vision

Create a working model of a cooperative, low cost, sustainable, resilient, debt free community dedicated to attracting young people, and the young at heart, back to America’s heartland and a kinder, gentler, more meaningful small town, locally oriented agrarian life.

core beliefs

  • Life in urban American environments is becoming untenable for people who seek to live the American ideal of “Life, Liberty and the Pursuit of Happiness.”
  • Young people are under a level of economic stress that is unprecedented in our lifetimes.
  • Education debt is at an all-time high, greater than all credit card debt in the USA.
  • High paying jobs for which young people have been educated, at great cost, are far more rare than the candidates that can fill the positions.
  • Purchasing a home has become out of reach for most young people in their 20’s and 30’s, if ever.
  • There is a growing cadre of young people who seek to relocate from high stress urban areas to far more human scale towns and villages in the country, to a kinder gentler way of living.
  • Using an LLC it is possible to create an entity that can be independent of its members’ financial and liability issues and its members can be independent of the financial and liability issues of the LLC.

silent fire ranch (sfr)

SFR is owned by SDCC LLC, a Wyoming LLC established in March of 2012 with the intention of the creation of the SFR and other communities in an approach towards cooperative living. SFR now has eight members and can expand up to 40 or 50 members over time.

SFR is an 81 acre ranch in Majors Ranch Subdivision in Huerfano County Colorado. This is the site of our demonstration project of creating a working cooperative example of a small agrarian village, where in time, and with the acquisition of two adjoining ranches, and the subdivision of SFR we will have the ability to construct up to 8 homes total by its members. One home is allowable by right on ranches of 35 acres or more, plus a second home can be allowed with a “Use Permit” authorized by the Huerfano County Commissioners. Typically these “Use Permits” have almost always been granted.

Beginning in 2015, SFR will be used for organized hands-on demonstration workshops where a variety of earth structures will be constructed by members and interns. Huerfano County represents a unique opportunity to live sustainable, low cost, debt free lives in the natural splendor. Land costs are downright cheap with lots selling for as little as $300/acre for larger parcels.
