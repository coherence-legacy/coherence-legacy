neuromuscular release

Neuromuscular release (including trigger-point work) addresses specific points of hyper-irritability in muscle tissue that can refer pain and restrict movement. The technique identifies these points and applies focused pressure to encourage the muscle to release its contraction and restore normal function.

finn’s way

I locate and work with neuromuscular points in areas including:

  • The deep layers of shoulder and upper back muscles
  • Pelvic floor release
  • TMJ

The work is precise and is always done within the client’s ability to remain relaxed. I do not force; I wait for the tissue to soften under sustained, attentive pressure. Neuromuscular therapy is often integrated with assisted stretching and joint mobilization for restorative, focused work on muscle contraction and pain.
