rolfing

Rolfing is a system of structural integration that works with the fascia and connective tissue to restore alignment and ease of movement. The work addresses patterns of holding that have developed over time and supports the body in finding a more balanced relationship with gravity.

finn’s way

While I am not a certified Rolfer, I have received extensive Rolfing and incorporate elements of structural awareness into my work. I pay attention to how the body organizes itself in gravity and support clients in releasing patterns that no longer serve them. The emphasis is always on the client’s own capacity to reorganize rather than on imposing a new structure. Structural awareness informs how I approach deep tissue, myofascial release, and neuromuscular work.
