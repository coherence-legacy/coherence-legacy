shiatsu energy session

Shiatsu means “finger pressure” in Japanese. Using a series of techniques, practitioners apply rhythmic finger pressure at specific points on the body in order to stimulate chi (vital energy), and stretches to release blockages on the body’s energy pathways (meridians).

underlying principles

Shiatsu works with the meridians — the energy pathways of the body — to support the free flow of chi. When energy is blocked or stagnant, physical and emotional symptoms can arise. Rhythmic pressure and stretching help restore balance.

what to expect in a session

The client remains clothed and lies on a mat or table. The practitioner applies rhythmic finger, palm, or elbow pressure along the meridians and uses stretches to open pathways. Sessions are quiet and focused.

finn’s way

In a Shiatsu energy session I work with the meridians through rhythmic pressure and stretching, supporting the free flow of energy. The session is quiet and focused. Clients often experience a deep sense of being reset — both physically and energetically — by the end of the session.
