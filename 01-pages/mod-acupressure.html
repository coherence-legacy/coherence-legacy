acupressure

Acupressure uses finger pressure on specific points along the body’s energy meridians to support the free flow of chi and to address patterns of tension, pain, or imbalance. It is a precise and non-invasive way of working with the body’s own regulatory systems.

finn’s way

I integrate acupressure points into longer sessions, holding points with steady, attentive pressure while remaining aware of the client’s breath and overall state. The work is always in service of the larger session rather than applied as a separate technique. Points are held long enough for the client to feel a shift — a softening, a release, or a sense of energy moving.
