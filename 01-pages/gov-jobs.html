Jobs at Silent Fire Ranch and The Merc

At Silent Fire Ranch and The Merc, residents and members obtain income in a variety of ways. At this time, there are still not many full-time jobs available until funds are available. Individuals and groups at Silent Fire Ranch and The Merc are in the developing stages of creating businesses within the SFR/TM community and there are a variety of ways for individuals to cobble together work inside and outside the SFR/TM community in order to get their financial needs met. Below is a list of some of the ways that folks have made money at Silent Fire Ranch and The Merc:

Residential/Commercial Construction

Market Gardening

Produce Harvesting

Creating Value-Added Foods

Direct Marketing

Teaching Locally

Online Education

Online Sales of Arts and Crafts

Within Silent Fire Ranch and The Merc, positions are potentially available for:

Administrative workers, for managerial, accounting, fundraising, grant writing, and article writing tasks

People excited about natural building, with or without construction experience

Construction managers

Experienced maintenance workers

Gardeners who are interested in growing organic vegetables

Biodiesel researchers and producers

Biogas researchers

Jobs currently open for applications are:

Huerfano Guide Manager

Grant Writer/Fund raiser

Personal assistant to Managing Manager

Farm Manager (SFR and Mill House?)

Contact us to check on the availability of these and other positions available to members and residents of Silent Fire Ranch and The Merc.
