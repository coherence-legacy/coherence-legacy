levels of consciousness

by Steve Pavlina — stevepavlina.com

In the book Power vs. Force by David R. Hawkins, there’s a hierarchy of levels of human consciousness. From low to high, the levels are: shame, guilt, apathy, grief, fear, desire, anger, pride, courage, neutrality, willingness, acceptance, reason, love, joy, peace, enlightenment.

While we can pop in and out of different levels at various times, usually there’s a predominant “normal” state for us. An increase from one level to another will result in enormous change in your life.

  • Shame — Just a step above death. Self-directed hatred.
  • Guilt — A step above shame. You think of yourself as a sinner.
  • Apathy — Feeling hopeless or victimized. Learned helplessness.
  • Grief — A state of perpetual sadness and loss. Depression.
  • Fear — Seeing the world as dangerous and unsafe. Paranoia.
  • Desire — The level of addiction, craving, and lust. Consumerism. Materialism.
  • Anger — Frustration, often from not having desires met. Can spur action or keep you stuck.
  • Pride — The first level where you start to feel good, but it’s a false feeling dependent on external circumstances.
  • Courage — The first level of true strength. Life becomes challenging and exciting instead of overwhelming.
  • Neutrality — “Live and let live.” Flexible, relaxed, unattached. The level of complacency.
  • Willingness — You start using your energy more effectively. Development of willpower and self-discipline.
  • Acceptance — You awaken to the possibilities of living proactively. Setting and achieving goals.
  • Reason — You transcend the emotional aspects and begin to think clearly and rationally. The level of medicine and science.
  • Love — Unconditional love, a permanent understanding of your connectedness with all that exists. Lifetime service to humanity.
  • Joy — A state of pervasive, unshakable happiness. The level of saints and advanced spiritual teachers.
  • Peace — Total transcendence. Extremely rare.
  • Enlightenment — The highest level, where humanity blends with divinity. The level of Krishna, Buddha, and Jesus.

Progressing from one level to the next requires an enormous amount of energy. The most important work we can do as human beings is to raise our individual level of consciousness. When we do this, we spread higher levels of consciousness to everyone around us.
