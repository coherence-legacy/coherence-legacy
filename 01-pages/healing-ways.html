an unprecedented opportunity

We are living in an unprecedented time in history, a time when humanity is awakening to itself. Awakening is about recognizing consciousness or presence that is beyond the mind. This awakening results in a lasting sense of deep inner peace. There are more teachers, books, and teachings pointing to this truth than ever before in history. People all over the globe are speaking of this awakening using many different words and methods. All are a part of this new consciousness.

True and lasting healing is dependent upon this awakening. We are not separate from our earth or from each other, even if we pretend that we are. As we awaken we begin to recognize our connectedness to all things, feel peaceful, and begin living life from a completely different place.

my hands

Like energy and spirit, my hands are also imbued with a quality that is not of my own making. They know healing absent my mental control over them.

In practice over a five year period of time I was often in a position of not knowing just what to do or how to do it or I just did not feel it mentally. My hands always knew what to do whenever I just let them lead. They still do.

As with Jeffrey Allen’s teaching, once you move the energy to where it needs to go, you let it do its things. You can’t and won’t know how or what it is doing and you don’t need to. Just know it will… every time.

Of course trusting spirit is much the same. Who can know the mind of God? But can we trust when we allow spirit to take the reins.

It is well known that craftsmen and women (if you are one) make good healers. They know how to work with their hands.
