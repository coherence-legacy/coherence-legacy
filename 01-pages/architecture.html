architecture

“You cannot overcome the land.” Working from the land inward… I design “backwards.” All is in answer to the land, the sun, wind, views and the flow of energy.

I have been fortunate to have had a remarkable set of clients over the forty-five years of providing architectural, interior design, art and antique sourcing from all over the world. Together with our amazing clients we created remarkable one-of-a-kind homes.

The projects in this Architecture page of my legacy site are of projects for which we provided architectural design services. In many cases we also provided novel project and construction management services in which we set up a construction entity for the owner as owner/builder and then we managed a singularly focused team of professional working singularly on our client’s project.

Our clients only needed to talk to one person, me, and then all else was taken care of by my teams. From initial property searches and feasibilities studies, to site design, site placement, landscape and vineyard planning, architectural design, engineering, art acquisition, interior design and the final move in and commissioning. Sometimes taking care of every detail of the home environment down to sheets, towels, books in the library. Even sometimes the design of clothes for special events that celebrated the owners relationship to the home.

There are many significant projects that were executed under non-disclosure agreements and they are not represented here. A couple of the projects have been listed for sale with excellent photography and videos presented publicly that we have access to at my atelier. They can be viewed if interested on a by-appointment basis.

These selected projects show a small representation of the diversity and scale of projects. Enjoy…….
