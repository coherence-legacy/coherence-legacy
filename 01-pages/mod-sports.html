sports massage

Sports Massage is a special focused massage that is typically used before, during, and after athletic events, to prepare the athlete for peak performance, drain away fatigue, relieve swelling, reduce muscle tension, promote flexibility, and to prevent injuries. I understand the process of athletic training and competition, the physiology of muscle function, what happens when someone trains and competes, and the kinesiology of movement for particular sports.

finn’s way

With athletes I focus on the specific demands of their sport and the patterns of tension that training creates. The work is both preparatory and restorative. Sessions support range of motion, full extension, and the ongoing maintenance of ease and flow so that the athlete can continue to train without accumulating injury. An ongoing system of work for athletes helps maintain maximum flow, ease of movement, and extension. Sessions are typically 1.5 hours.
