streaming light

Our clients had laid out a profoundly conscious and articulate view of just how they wanted to live their years beyond work and the hustle of life in the 21st century. At every level of consideration, many far beyond the material, they provided us with inspiration for just how amazing this home could be. And they understood the very dramatic wildness of their chosen site.

It was clear from the very first experience on the site that Streaming Light would be an example of place telling its aspiring co-creators just exactly what it wants. While integrating all the owner's vision and desires, this home was designed by the very nature of the ridge from which it grew; organically, from the earth and stone of the High Desert it inhabits.

In the spring and late fall, the winds howl over the mountains and ridges. The curvilinear form of the house and roof planes sloping down with the ridge allows the wind to slide around and over the house. The continuously curved walls provide wind-less sanctuaries, regardless of which direction the wind is blowing. The articulation of the exterior walls of glass provides a visual experience of the home in the setting of the natural landscape and stunning views.

Taking cues from the ancient Southwest vernacular and Anasazi mesa and cliff dwellings of the area, the curvilinear forms are punctuated with sixty massive 5–10 ton boulders that form outcroppings and natural features, bringing the surrounding mountains into the home. A small rock-lined rill honoring the precious mountain water that courses through the high desert valley floors runs through the Entry and Great Room.

The home sits on a ridge with a 360 degree view. In order to capture the expanses from each space, the design emerged without a single straight line. Streaming Light is made up of a series of nested cylinders of various diameters and heights, creating a dramatic monumental, yet intimate, interior living environment.

Standing in front of curved walls of glass in every space, you are surrounded by magnificent sunrises and sunsets, and dramatic thunderstorms charging across the immense sky. With 180 degree views, you feel as though you can reach out and touch the high desert, the even higher mountains and, to the east, the thousand-mile expanse of the Great Plains.

Our clients wanted to build a home made of the earth that would one day melt back into the earth. We probably failed miserably at this, as this 3,000,000 lb. home is likely to last for a thousand years. Its mass is made up of 28,000 compressed earth adobe blocks made from the earth directly below the building, anchored by sandstone boulders brought from the foot of the nearby mountains.

At the building site, every plant, tree, cactus, and creature that could be moved was carefully relocated. Any that were not directly under the construction were carefully preserved.

Using earth, stone, steel, timber and glass, Streaming Light is a unique expression of elegant crafted naturalness. All materials inside and out are raw, natural, elemental, handmade with precision and attention to quality and detail at every intersection.

Streaming Light was constructed at every level with a philosophy of “beyond green.” “Brown” would be the more appropriate description of what informs every cubic inch of Streaming Light’s magic and integrity.

And last but not least, Streaming Light is imbued with a vibrating, pulsing, living energy; the result of the place telling us so clearly and beautifully what it wanted. We think that this place agrees.
