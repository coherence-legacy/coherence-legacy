remote healing

I have only a small amount of experience of this, but I found it to be an extremely powerful, effective, and immediate way to work without physical limitation within my client’s bodies.

The standout session for me was working with a friend on her inner ear that had been completely engulfed in a tumor, likely from prolonged cell phone use. Using her energy frequency expressed in color, we entered the inner ear and washed the entire area in color that we could see affected various parts of the tumor. She could feel the energy at work later that day. When she took a shower she could hear her fingertips on her scalp as she washed her hair for the first time in months. She was already scheduled for laser surgery a few days later and then recovered fully.

When I fell out of the fifth wheel when we first arrived at Streaming Light Indian Camp, I spent several days using remote healing on my own knee and stitched back together a torn ACL.

I used this technique often with my partner to work on specific health issues, to remove energetic hitchhikers, and to connect energetically when we were apart. My experience with remote healing was remarkable. Through visualization I could enter my client’s bodies and work from within with complete mobility and inner vision. I could clearly see inside of bodies like a three-dimensional matrix. It was not “wet.” All was clear, separate, and accessible. With energy that was divine in nature, I could flood, saturate, massage, laser, manipulate, and possibly draw out energy.
