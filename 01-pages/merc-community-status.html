The Merc and Sangre de Cristo Community Update, January 2025

Happy 2015 to all of us…………..This is a very exciting year as so many opportunities are presenting themselves to us to manifest………or not. The possibilities are myriad!!!

This letter is a State of Sangre de Cristo Communities (SDCC) and The Merc Communities Letter. Things have evolved; indeed changed so much and so fast, that we need to get clear as a community on all the opportunities, needs and challenges that face us in this nascent stage of community.

We came here originally to create a sustainable, spiritual community at Majors Ranch with a group of us who mostly had adjacent properties and a strong conviction about creating a new way to live in cooperation and harmony with each other. It soon became clear that as a group we all had a different idea of what that looked like. We also quickly learned that there was no way to isolate ourselves in Majors Ranch, that in fact the community included the La Veta and Gardener Community as well. None of us believed that it would or could involve Walsenburg as it was too far gone.

Well, that has changed for us here at The Merc. This project was originally intended to be in support of our efforts at Silent Fire Ranch, but has now, in a matter of less than four months, taken on a whole new life of its own. We have been in The Merc now for three months and three weeks. Below we document all that we have been up to and plan now to continue with at The Merc:

the merc

We are fully moved into the Merc for the winter and are actively evolving its form and capacities for community daily.

When we first moved in November 1st, our first priority was to change the heating and hot water system to natural gas. Much of the piping was in place, but all the furnaces and boiler required repair and modification. Both water heaters had to be replaced. We are very glad we did as it has been very cold at times and the heating and electric bills are about what we have budgeted.

The workshop is up and running, at least the woodworking part of it. We will be moving the metal working shop to the basement level where it can be much bigger. There is still much fine tuning but we hope to have all the various shops including wood, metal, finishing, glass, ceramics in and fully functional by April. The team has been gathering tools, equipment and resources from local sources.

We have disassembled the huge Gerber fabric cutting machine on the Genius Level as there appeared to be no viable takers and the Genius Level needs to be fully functional ASAP. Mike Montoya our newest intern took over the disassembly process very systematically so that we could reuse, repurpose or sell the parts that include several electric motors up to 40 horse power!!! The frame of the machine which is massive has been cut into three parts to get them down the elevator and turned into three massive work tables.

Walls are built and sheet rocked on one side allowing access for electrical, sound insulation, inspection and final sheetrock and earth plaster to the walls.

sangre de cristo school for natural living (sdcs)

This is coming into sharp focus now as the New Year begins and we design programs which can start as early as this spring and summer. Last week SDCS was incorporated with the Colorado Secretary of State as a non-profit Educational institution. Next week we are filing for 501c3 status with the IRS.

As a non-profit 501c3 we will be eligible for numerous grants programs and be able to provide a way for donors to receive tax write-offs for contributions to SDCS. Currently we are seeking a top notch grant writer to assist us in identifying and supporting us through our first round of fund raising.

Immediately we are seeking $15,000 in tax deductible donor support to pay for legal and up front expenses as we get the structural work done to begin educational operations as soon as this summer.

st mary’s field

February 1st SDCS signed a 30 year lease for the old St Mary’s football field in Walsenburg along the Cucharas River on the south edge of Walsenburg at 9th and Hendren Streets. The football field is 1½ city blocks, really the size of two football fields and incorporates 18 city lots and the Tenth Street right of way, and an alley right of way both of which we are endeavoring to have the city vacate. The football field will be the Sangre de Cristo Community’s demonstration farm that will be the public face and facility for public outreach. It will also be an integral part of the educational opportunities for our interns and paying students.

Inspired by the Real Goods center in Hopland California and The Eden Project in Cornwall England, we are beginning to collaboratively design a Conceptual Master Plan for the development of the field which will include up to six earth and other alternative/natural building demonstration homes, permaculture design and systems, homesteading and off-grid living technologies, organic and bio-dynamic farming, natural water treatment, waste treatment, solar/wind/free energy, irrigation and water conservation.

businesses at the merc

Sangre de Cristo Design Group — Conceptual master planning and design services in partnership with PDF Design Inc. and Coherence LLC, with additional joint venture relationship with RMG (The Rocky Mountain Group).

Ecobana LLC — Cooperative entity to develop production use of the wood, metal, ceramic, glass, and stone workshops. Focused on a local approach to sourcing sustainable materials and craftspeople.

Yul Jorgensen, Artist in Residence — Huerfano County’s youngest nationally recognized artist, building out his art studio and helping manage The Merc as Vice Manager.

Matt Castile, Executive Chef — Joined in December 2014. Promoting excellent local food and farm-to-table lifestyle.

silent fire ranch

This year will be a very exciting year for the evolution of SFR. Prioritized projects include roads, tent sites, powerhouse, solar plant, organic/bio-dynamic garden, permaculture gardens, fencing, integrated water system, community kitchen, solar shower/bath house, composting toilet, barn workshop, intern casitas, Walipini greenhouse, meditation temple, and ceremonial kiva. Many of these will be offered as workshop classes through the Sangre de Cristo School for Natural Living.

creating a successful culture

We have a great group of young interns forming to create a wonderful community able to help mitigate many of the ills of our world today. A community that is in support of all of its interns and members to create a viable new paradigm for rural and country living — a “Rural Renaissance” including sustainability, resilience, self-reliance, local water, food, energy, natural building, economy, holistic health care, and self-governance.

Conditions for success include reasonable limits on consumption, living by the Field of Alliance agreements, ongoing disciplines (yoga, meditation, bodywork, mentoring), and strong financial backing with mature mentors willing to step in at a proactive management level.
